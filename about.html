Our Journey

How LittleSprout Began

LittleSprout was born from a mother's dream to give her baby the purest start in life without harming the planet. After struggling to find truly organic, plastic-free baby products, founder Sarah decided to create her own.

What started as a small Etsy shop in 2020 has grown into a beloved community of over 5,000 families who share our passion for sustainability, quality, and the well-being of children everywhere.

Today, we're proud to offer a full range of GOTS certified organic clothing, FSC certified wooden toys, zero-waste essentials, and monthly subscription boxes — all shipped in 100% plastic-free packaging.
