Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3-7 business days. Express shipping (2-3 days) is available at checkout.

What's your return policy?

We offer 30-day returns on all unused products in original packaging. Subscription boxes can be paused or canceled anytime.

Are your products really organic?

Yes! All our clothing is GOTS certified organic cotton. Our wooden toys are FSC certified with non-toxic, water-based paints.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes! All orders come in our signature eco-friendly gift packaging. You can also add a personalized gift message at checkout.
