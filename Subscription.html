Frequently Asked Questions

Can I skip or cancel my subscription?

Yes! You can pause, skip, or cancel your subscription anytime from your account dashboard. No hidden fees.

Absolutely! All clothing is GOTS certified organic cotton, toys are FSC certified wood with non-toxic paints, and packaging is 100% plastic-free and compostable.

Your first box ships within 3-5 business days. Subsequent boxes ship on the same date each month.
