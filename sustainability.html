All our fabrics are GOTS certified organic cotton. Our wood is FSC certified from responsibly managed forests.

100% plastic-free, compostable, and recyclable packaging. No bubble wrap, no plastic tags — ever.

Ethical Production

Fair wages, safe working conditions, and partnerships with B Corp certified manufacturers.
